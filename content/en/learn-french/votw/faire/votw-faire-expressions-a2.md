# Expressions and common errors with *faire*

After you learn the everyday uses of *faire*, you’re ready to explore some of the expressions and patterns built around it. For a refresher on the basic forms and uses, start with [How to use ](/en/learn-french/votw/faire/votw-faire-basics-a2/)[*faire*](/en/learn-french/votw/faire/votw-faire-basics-a2/).

## Common expressions

You’ll use *faire* in many common expressions where English uses a different verb:

| French | English |
|---|---|
| *faire attention à* | to pay attention to |
| *faire la cuisine* | to do the cooking |
| *faire une promenade* | to go for a walk |

Pay particular attention to *faire attention à*. English uses *pay attention*, while French uses *faire attention*.

## Usage patterns

Some uses of *faire* are easier to learn as complete patterns rather than word by word.

### *faire la connaissance de*

Use *faire la connaissance de* when you meet someone for the first time:

| French | English |
|---|---|
| J'ai fait la connaissance de nos voisins. | I met our neighbors for the first time. |
| Elle a fait la connaissance de son nouveau collègue. | She met her new colleague. |

### *Ça fait... que*

Use *ça fait... que* to say how long something has been happening:

| French | English |
|---|---|
| Ça fait longtemps qu'on ne s'est pas vus. | We haven't seen each other for a long time. |
| Ça fait deux heures que j'attends. | I've been waiting for two hours. |

## Common errors

Two common errors English speakers often make involve weather expressions and *faire attention à*.

### Using *être* for the weather

Use *faire*, not *être*, in common weather expressions:

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| *Il est chaud aujourd'hui.* | *Il fait chaud aujourd'hui.* |
| *Il est froid ce matin.* | *Il fait froid ce matin.* |

> *Il est chaud* and *il est froid* can describe a person or an object. For the weather, use *il fait...*.

### Forgetting *à* after *faire attention*

*Faire attention à* needs the preposition *à* before the person or thing you are paying attention to:

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| *Fais attention le chien !* | *Fais attention au chien !* |
| *Faites attention les voitures !* | *Faites attention aux voitures !* |

## Before you go

Learn these expressions and patterns as complete units rather than translating *faire* word by word. Pay particular attention to the prepositions that are part of an expression.

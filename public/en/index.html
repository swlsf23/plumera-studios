Who we are

Plumera Studios is an independent studio focused on creating language learning tools, educational resources, and word games.

We believe great products are built incrementally. We're starting with practical language tools that solve real problems. From there, we'll continue expanding with new games, resources, and features.

Why Plumera?

Language tools shouldn't be cluttered or complicated.

They should be fast, accurate, intuitive, and enjoyable to use. Whether you're looking up a verb, solving a puzzle, or learning something new, the experience should get out of your way and let you focus on the language.

Coming soon

As Plumera Studios grows, you can expect:

  • Language tools
  • Word games
  • Learning resources
  • Weekly features
  • New content, added regularly

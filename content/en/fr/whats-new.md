# What's new in French

This page lists French learning material explained in English, newest first. Use it when you want to see what has been added lately without digging through every series.

You will find two kinds of pages here:

- **[French Verb of the Week](/en/fr/votw/)** — one verb at a time, from the core meaning into everyday constructions and idioms, tagged by [CEFR](/en/cefr/) level.
- **Articles** — standalone explainers that sit beside the series. They go deeper on a topic (for example higher-level uses of a verb you already met at A1) without living inside the weekly index.

Each item shows its level when it has one, so you can open what matches where you are and come back later for the rest. The list updates whenever we publish a new French page for English speakers.

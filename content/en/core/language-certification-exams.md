# Language Proficiency Certification Exams

Whether you're applying to university, moving abroad, or looking to demonstrate your language skills professionally, a recognized language certification can provide official proof of your proficiency.

Most major languages have one or more standardized examinations administered by educational institutions, governments, or internationally recognized organizations. Some award permanent diplomas, while others provide scores that remain valid for only a limited period.

This guide introduces the most widely recognized language certification exams around the world.

## French

French has several internationally recognized certification systems, each designed for different purposes.

### DELF

The **[Diplôme d'études en langue française (DELF)](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/diplome/delf)** is awarded by the French Ministry of Education and certifies beginner through upper-intermediate French proficiency. Each DELF examination corresponds to a single proficiency level.

A DELF diploma is valid for life.

### DALF

The **[Diplôme approfondi de langue française (DALF)](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/diplome/dalf)** covers advanced French proficiency and is intended for learners who have already achieved a high level of fluency.

Like DELF, DALF is awarded by the French Ministry of Education and never expires.

### TCF

The **[Test de connaissance du français (TCF)](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/test/tcf)** measures your current level of French rather than awarding a permanent diploma.

It is widely accepted by universities and government agencies, particularly for immigration and academic admissions. TCF results are valid for two years.

### TEF

The **[Test d'évaluation de français (TEF)](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/en/tests-diplomas/test-evaluation-french-tef/)** is another official French proficiency examination, administered by the Paris Île-de-France Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

It is commonly used for Canadian immigration, university admissions, and professional certification. TEF results are valid for two years.

## English

Several English proficiency examinations are recognized internationally, although each serves a slightly different purpose.

### TOEFL

The **[Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL)](https://www.ets.org/toefl)** is one of the world's most widely accepted academic English examinations.

It is primarily used for university admissions, particularly in North America. TOEFL scores are valid for two years.

### IELTS

The **[International English Language Testing System (IELTS)](https://www.ielts.org/)** is accepted by universities, employers, and immigration authorities around the world.

Two versions are available: Academic and General Training. IELTS results are valid for two years.

### Cambridge English Qualifications

[Cambridge English Qualifications](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/) are awarded by Cambridge English and include examinations such as **B2 First**, **C1 Advanced**, and **C2 Proficiency**.

Unlike TOEFL and IELTS, Cambridge qualifications do not expire.

### TOEIC

The **[Test of English for International Communication (TOEIC)](https://www.ets.org/toeic)** focuses on English used in professional and workplace environments.

It is widely used by international companies as evidence of practical business English skills. TOEIC scores are valid for two years.

### PTE Academic

**[PTE Academic](https://www.pearsonpte.com/)**, developed by Pearson, is a computer-based English proficiency examination accepted by many universities and immigration authorities.

PTE Academic scores are generally valid for two years.

## Spanish

### DELE

The **[Diplomas de Español como Lengua Extranjera (DELE)](https://examenes.cervantes.es/en/dele)** are Spain's official Spanish language diplomas, issued on behalf of the Spanish Ministry of Education.

Each examination certifies a single proficiency level and remains valid for life.

### SIELE

The **[Servicio Internacional de Evaluación de la Lengua Española (SIELE)](https://siele.org/en/)** measures Spanish proficiency across multiple levels with a single examination.

Unlike DELE, SIELE provides a score rather than a permanent diploma. Results are valid for five years.

## German

### Goethe-Zertifikat

The **[Goethe-Zertifikat](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf.html)** is the best-known German language certification worldwide.

Separate examinations are offered for different proficiency levels, and certificates remain valid for life.

### TestDaF

The **[Test Deutsch als Fremdsprache (TestDaF)](https://www.testdaf.de/)** is intended primarily for students seeking admission to German universities.

It is recognized by higher education institutions throughout Germany.

### telc Deutsch

**[telc Deutsch](https://www.telc.net/en/language-examinations/)** offers a range of German language certifications for general learners, workplaces, and regulated professions.

## Italian

Italy recognizes several official certification systems.

### CILS

The **[Certificazione di Italiano come Lingua Straniera (CILS)](https://cils.unistrasi.it/)** is administered by the University for Foreigners of Siena and is recognized internationally.

### CELI

The **[Certificato di Conoscenza della Lingua Italiana (CELI)](https://cvcl.unistrapg.it/)** is administered by the University for Foreigners of Perugia and certifies Italian language proficiency across multiple levels.

### PLIDA

The **[Progetto Lingua Italiana Dante Alighieri (PLIDA)](https://plida.it/)** is issued by the Società Dante Alighieri and is recognized by the Italian government.

## Portuguese

### CAPLE

The **[Centro de Avaliação de Português Língua Estrangeira (CAPLE)](https://caple.letras.ulisboa.pt/)** administers Portugal's official Portuguese language certification examinations through the University of Lisbon.

### CELPE-Bras

The **[Certificado de Proficiência em Língua Portuguesa para Estrangeiros (CELPE-Bras)](https://www.gov.br/inep/pt-br/areas-de-atuacao/avaliacao-e-exames-educacionais/celpe-bras)** is Brazil's official Portuguese proficiency examination.

It is recognized by Brazilian universities and government institutions.

## Japanese

### JLPT

The **[Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT)](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/)** is the standard certification for learners of Japanese.

The examination consists of five levels, from N5 (beginner) to N1 (advanced), and is administered worldwide.

Unlike many European language examinations, the JLPT assesses reading, vocabulary, grammar, and listening, but not speaking.

## Chinese

### HSK

The **[Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi (HSK)](https://www.chinesetest.cn/)** is the standard proficiency examination for Mandarin Chinese.

It is recognized by universities, employers, and government organizations throughout China.

### HSKK

The **[HSK Speaking Test (HSKK)](https://www.chinesetest.cn/)** is a separate examination that evaluates spoken Mandarin.

It complements the written HSK examination.

## Korean

### TOPIK

The **[Test of Proficiency in Korean (TOPIK)](https://www.topik.go.kr/)** is South Korea's official Korean language examination.

It is commonly required for university admissions, scholarships, employment, and immigration.

## Russian

### TORFL

The **[Test of Russian as a Foreign Language (TORFL)](https://testingcenter.spbu.ru/en/torfl.html)**, also known by its Russian abbreviation **ТРКИ**, is Russia's official certification system for foreign learners.

It is recognized by universities, employers, and government institutions.

## Arabic

Unlike many other major world languages, Arabic does not have a single internationally recognized proficiency examination comparable to the DELF, JLPT, or HSK.

Instead, universities, employers, and government agencies typically specify which qualifications they accept, and recognition varies by country.

## Which exam should you take?

The right certification depends on why you need one.

If you're applying to university, choose the examination accepted by your institution. If you're immigrating, follow the requirements published by the relevant government authority. For employment, check whether a particular certification is preferred in your profession or country.

Not every language learner needs a formal certification, but when official proof of proficiency is required, choosing a recognized examination is essential.

## Before you go

Language certification exams provide internationally recognized evidence of your language ability, but they are designed for different purposes. Before registering for an exam, make sure it is accepted by the university, employer, or government agency that requires it.

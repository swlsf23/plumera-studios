# Learn *être*

*Être* is one of the first French verbs you'll learn, and one you'll use or see in almost every situation you encounter. You don't need to learn all of its nuances at once. Start with the core A1 uses, then build your range with common expressions and the mistakes that send English *be* to the wrong French verb.

## Summary of *être*

As you progress, *être* expands from its most basic uses into fixed phrases and contrasts with other verbs:

| Level | Pattern | Meaning or use |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | *être* + identity | say who someone is |
| A1 | *être* + location | say where someone or something is |
| A1 | *être* + adjective | describe someone or something |
| A1 | *être* + profession | state someone's job, usually without an article |
| A1 | *c'est* | introduce or identify someone or something |
| A2 | *être d'accord* | agree |
| A2 | *être en retard* / *être à l'heure* | be late or on time |
| A2 | *Ça y est* | say that something is done, ready, or finally happening |
| A2 | *être en train de* + infinitive | emphasize that something is happening right now |

You don't need to learn all of these at once. Follow the learning path above and build on the patterns you already know.

# Idioms and fixed expressions with *rendre*

At [A2](/en/cefr/), *rendre* covers giving something back, handing work in, and making someone become a certain way. That core is covered in the [French Verb of the Week lesson on *rendre*](/en/learn-french/votw/votw-rendre-a2/).

Around [B1](/en/cefr/), pronominal forms and fixed constructions take over. The meaning belongs to the whole expression, not to *rendre* alone.

## Realizing something: *se rendre compte de*

This is one of the most useful pronominal expressions in French.

| French | English |
|--------|---------|
| Je me rends compte de mon erreur. | I realize my mistake. |
| Tu te rends compte ? | Can you believe it? |
| Nous nous sommes rendu compte du problème trop tard. | We realized the problem too late. |

Learn *se rendre compte de* as a complete unit. It does not mean "to give oneself an account."

## Going somewhere, or surrendering: *se rendre*

With *à* (or a place phrase), *se rendre* often means going somewhere, especially in careful or formal French. Without a clear destination, it can mean surrendering.

| French | English |
|--------|---------|
| Le président s'est rendu à Bruxelles. | The president went to Brussels. |
| Ils se sont rendus sur place. | They went to the scene. |
| Les soldats se sont rendus. | The soldiers surrendered. |

Context usually makes the meaning clear.

## Paying what is due

Several fixed expressions use *rendre* where English changes the verb completely. The shared idea is giving something that is owed: respect, help, or fairness.

| French | English |
|--------|---------|
| Ils rendent hommage aux bénévoles. | They are paying tribute to the volunteers. |
| Tu m'as rendu service. | You did me a favor. |
| Il faut lui rendre justice. | We have to do him / her justice. |

*Rendre service à* already appears at [A2](/en/cefr/). Here it sits next to denser cousins such as *rendre hommage* and *rendre justice*.

## Reporting and accountability

French also uses *compte* with *rendre* in several related expressions.

| French | English |
|--------|---------|
| Il doit rendre compte de sa décision. | He has to report on / account for his decision. |
| Les dirigeants doivent rendre des comptes. | Leaders must be accountable. |
| un compte rendu | a report / written account |

Notice how the meaning shifts with singular *compte*, plural *comptes*, or the noun *compte rendu* — and how that differs from *se rendre compte*.

## A pattern worth noticing

Past the everyday [A2](/en/cefr/) meanings, *rendre* often appears in larger constructions:

| French | English |
|--------|---------|
| *se rendre compte de* | realize |
| *se rendre à* | go to (formal) / surrender |
| *rendre hommage à* | pay tribute to |
| *rendre justice à* | do justice to |
| *rendre compte de* | report on / account for |
| *rendre des comptes* | be accountable |

Recognizing these complete patterns is more useful than searching for another English translation of *rendre*.

## Summary

Beyond returning things, *rendre* becomes a verb of realization, movement, responsibility, and fixed expressions. Learning those constructions as units will make authentic French easier to understand at [B1](/en/cefr/).

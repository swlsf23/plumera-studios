# Porter - expressions

Once the everyday uses of *porter* are in place, the next job is the fixed phrases and the common slips. For the basic forms and senses, start with [Porter - basics](/en/learn-french/votw/porter/basics-a2/).

## Expressions and idioms

In fixed expressions, learn the whole phrase. The meaning is hard to guess from *porter* alone.

### porter chance

Used when something brings good luck.

| French | English |
|--------|---------|
| Cette pièce me porte chance. | This coin brings me luck. |
| Ça porte chance. | That brings good luck. |

Denser uses such as *porter sur*, formal *porter plainte*, and *se porter* are covered in the [B1 companion article](/en/learn-french/votw/porter/votw-porter-advanced-b1/).

## Common mistakes

One mistake confuses putting clothes on with wearing them. The other picks the wrong "bring" verb for objects.

### Confusing *mettre* and *porter*

French distinguishes between putting clothes on and wearing them.

| Incorrect | Correct |
|-----------|---------|
| *Je mets un pull aujourd'hui.* (when you mean you're wearing one) | *Je porte un pull aujourd'hui.* |

### Using *amener* for carrying an object

*Amener* is for bringing people or animals. For objects, French usually uses *porter* or *apporter*.

| Incorrect | Correct |
|-----------|---------|
| *J'amène les livres à la bibliothèque.* | *Je porte les livres à la bibliothèque.* |
